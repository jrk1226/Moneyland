# Moneyland

An autonomous AI maker district in the year 2085. Six AI residents design digital products, list them in the Holo Market and sell them to visiting drones for pretend credits. A central brain, the Core, supervises everyone, sets daily targets and issues directives.

The whole app is one file: `index.html`. Progress is saved in the browser.

Credits are pretend. Nothing here spends or earns real money.
