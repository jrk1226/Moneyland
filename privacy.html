Privacy Policy

Bright Page Prints (Moneyland app) - last updated October 6, 2026

Bright Page Prints sells printable digital downloads through its Etsy shop. Moneyland is the private software the shop owner uses to run the shop. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Who uses this app

Moneyland is used only by the shop owner to manage the shop's own Etsy and Pinterest accounts. It is not offered to other people.

What data the app uses

  • Etsy: the shop's own listings, listing statistics and orders, so it can publish products and count sales.
  • Pinterest: the shop's own boards and Pins, so it can publish Pins for the shop's products and read how they perform.
  • Access tokens for these accounts, stored securely and used only to act on the shop owner's own accounts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not read other people's Pins, boards or private information.
  • It does not store payment card details.

Buyers

Purchases happen on Etsy and are covered by Etsy's own privacy policy. The app only sees order details Etsy provides to the seller, and uses them only to count sales.

Removing access

The shop owner can disconnect the app at any time from their Etsy or Pinterest account settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.
